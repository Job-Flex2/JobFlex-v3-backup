# The AI desk

The company's number, answered: a virtual receptionist that gives callers what they ask, books visits and takes messages — or the company phone rings first and the desk takes what nobody picked up. Built in steps from 2026-10-13 (owner: "AI desk to answer client calls, give info and schedule appointments").

## One number for calls and texts (step 1)

The toll-free number `lib/sms/registration` buys for a company answers calls the day it is bought; texting waits for the carriers' approval. `docs/sms.md` → "One number for calls and texts" has the routing (`lib/aiDesk/numbers.ts`, `lib/aiDesk/calls.ts`, the two voice webhooks) and the settings card's words.

## What the desk knows and says (step 2)

Settings → **AI desk** (desk pane `settings-blueprint/panes/desk-pane.tsx`; the handheld settings page has the same two cards; the Phone page links here).

- **Who answers** (`components/v3/ai-desk/desk-setup.tsx` `DeskStatus`): the number and where its calls go, and two buttons — *The desk answers every call* / *Ring my phone first* — `actions/aiDesk setDeskMode` → `SyncState aidesk:<orgId>` (`lib/aiDesk/shared.ts` DeskSettings; no schema change). A plan without the Phone page is told calls ring the company phone.
- **Tell us about your company** (`DeskSetup`): a free-text brief, typed or dictated (`DictateButton`), with chips of what JobFlex already knows. *Write the desk's instructions* → `composeDeskProfile`: `lib/aiDesk/facts.ts companyFacts` (name, phone, address → city, website, trades, booking hours in words, booked services with prices, the booking page) + the brief → `lib/aiDesk/profile.ts composePrompt` → the model (JSON) → `cleanCompose` (checked against `profileFromFacts`, the facts-only draft) → saved with the brief, the questions the desk still has (the model's + `gapQuestions`) and three sample answers. With nothing written, the page shows the facts-only draft.
- **The instructions, section by section** (`PROFILE_SECTIONS`: greeting, about, services, service area, hours, prices, credentials, FAQs, emergencies, never-say, transfer, languages): editable; `saveDeskProfile` → `cleanProfile`. The greeting must say *virtual assistant* and that the call is *recorded* (WA is an all-party-consent state).
- **Try it**: `askDesk(question)` answers from `deskInstructions(profile, facts, mode)` — the system prompt the live desk will run on (step 3): identity and tone, every section, booking only from the calendar's open times, messages, the never list, languages.
- Checks: `scripts/qa/ai-desk-profile.check.ts` (pure, 17), `scripts/qa/sms.check.ts` (the settings' parse), the stand walk `walk-desk2.js` (desk + phone, the fake model answers the two prompts).
- Rate limits per company: 30 rewrites and 60 try-it questions an hour.

## The live desk (step 3)

`voice/` — a small always-on Node service on Fly.io (`voice/README.md` has the owner's steps: Fly account, secrets, `voice.jobflex.app`, two Vercel variables). Twilio ↔ OpenAI realtime ↔ JobFlex's internal API; no Twilio credentials, no database.

- **The call.** With `VOICE_SERVER_URL` + `VOICE_SERVER_SECRET` set, the assistant's TwiML is `<Connect><Stream url=…/twilio/stream><Parameter name="token"/></Stream></Connect><Redirect>/api/twilio/voice/after</Redirect>` (`streamTwiml`); the token is an HMAC of {callSid, organizationId, from, exp} (`lib/aiDesk/voiceToken.ts`). The server (`voice/call.mjs`) posts the token to `/api/desk/call-brief`, opens the realtime session (μ-law both ways, server VAD, input transcription, five tools), has the desk greet first, passes audio straight through, clears Twilio's queue when the caller talks over the desk, and runs the tools against the app. When the call ends it posts `/api/desk/call-end` (transcript, outcome, seconds, what was booked) and closes; Twilio then asks `/api/twilio/voice/after`: outcome *done* → `<Hangup/>`, *transfer* → `<Dial>` the company phone (a no-answer there goes to the old recording, never the live desk again), anything else (the desk never spoke) → "Our assistant can't take the call right now" + a recording.
- **The internal API** (`src/app/api/desk/*`, `lib/aiDesk/liveCall.ts`): header `x-desk-secret` (constant-time) + the call token on every request. `call-brief` = `deskInstructions(profile, facts, mode)` + today's date/time in the company's zone, the caller's number, and the client the shop knows by it (greeted by name once confirmed); the voice (`DeskSettings.voice`, default marin); a 10-minute cap. `open-times` picks the booked service that fits the caller's words (leak/no heat → the urgent diagnostic; tune-up; else the estimate), two slots a day, six at most, ids `serviceKey|ISO`. `book` → `createBooking` (the same code as the booking page: client/lead, appointment, Booking row, office text, customer email); `message` → a Lead (source phone) + trail; `call-end` → AiPhoneCall completed with duration, the transcript ("Desk: …/Caller: …") summarized as before (`attachTranscript(…, { lead: false })` when the desk already booked or took a message), the outcome in `SyncState callout:<callSid>` (no schema change).
- **Tests.** `cd voice && npm test` (5: booked call, transfer, message, refused token, the beta session shape) against fakes in `voice/test/fakes.mjs` — a scripted realtime API, a fake app, a fake Twilio stream client. Stand walk `walk-voice.js` (16): the real voice server + the real app and booking code with the fake realtime — appointment, Booking, transcript, outcome, hang-up/dial/recording.
- **Not verified yet:** the real OpenAI realtime protocol (the GA session shape is used; `REALTIME_API=beta` switches to the older one) and real Twilio audio — the first live call on the test number is step 6.

## The voice, the call log, "test it here" (step 4)

- **The voice** (`DeskStatus`): a picker of OpenAI's realtime voices (`lib/aiDesk/shared VOICES`, each in a few words — marin recommended) → `setDeskVoice`; *Hear it* → `previewDeskVoice`: the saved greeting through `gpt-4o-mini-tts` in that voice, an MP3 the page plays (40 an hour per company). The live desk reads `DeskSettings.voice` on every call (`callBrief`).
- **The month's minutes** (`lib/aiDesk/usage.ts`): one `SyncState deskuse:<orgId>:<YYYY-MM>` row — `{ callSeconds, calls, testSeconds, tests }` — added to by `finishCall` (a live call) and `endDeskTest` (a browser test); UTC months. Shown on the settings card ("4 min this month — 3 calls, 1 test"), the Phone page's line and its fourth stat card. Step 5 counts the plan's included minutes and the packs against this row. The spend is noted as `openai realtime-minute` / `tts-preview` (`lib/paidApiCosts`).
- **The Phone page**: a line under the title (`lib/aiDesk/strip.ts` → `PhoneDeskStrip`, words in `deskStripWords`): the number, who answers (and, until `VOICE_SERVER_URL` is set, that callers are asked to leave a message), the voice, the minutes, *Test it here*. Each call the desk took carries what it did — *booked a visit · took a message · handed to you · answered · couldn't take it* (`deskOutcomeWord` from the `callout:<sid>` row; the loader reads them in one query, no schema change) — an *AI desk* filter, and in the sheet a section with the seconds on the line and *Open the visit* → `/dashboard/calendar?date=YYYY-MM-DD&ev=apt:<id>` (the calendar's new `?ev=` entry param opens that event's sheet on arrival and clears the address bar). Transcript lines written "Desk: …" show as *AI desk*.
- **Test it here** (`components/v3/ai-desk/desk-test.tsx`, pure parts in `lib/aiDesk/testCall.ts`): the owner calls the desk from the browser. `startDeskTest` mints a one-session key from OpenAI (`POST /v1/realtime/client_secrets`, the session carrying the desk's instructions + `TEST_NOTE`, the voice, input transcription, server turn-taking and the five tools — `DESK_TOOLS`, kept identical to the voice server's `TOOLS`; `REALTIME_API=beta` uses `/realtime/sessions`); the browser opens a WebRTC peer connection to `https://api.openai.com/v1/realtime/calls` with that key (microphone track out, the desk's audio in, events on the `oai-events` data channel), has the desk greet first, and runs each tool through `deskTestTool`: **open times are the calendar's real ones; booking, messages and transfers only say what would happen** ("The desk would book Sam for Thursday at 10 AM — a test, nothing saved"). The panel shows the call as a two-sided transcript with those notes; *Hang up*, or the desk's own goodbye, ends it; 5-minute cap; `endDeskTest` counts the seconds. 20 test calls an hour per company. The model is `VOICE_MODEL` (default `gpt-realtime-mini`) — set it on Vercel as well as on Fly to change it in both places.
- Checks: `scripts/qa/ai-desk-step4.check.ts` (pure, 29: the data-channel events, the session shapes, the tool lists match, minutes, outcome words, the Phone line); stand walk `walk-desk4.js` (the Phone page with seeded desk calls, the visit opened on the calendar, the voice saved and heard, a test call driven by a fake peer connection against the real app — nothing booked, the minutes counted).
- **Not verified yet:** a real WebRTC session (the GA shapes follow OpenAI's current guide; `REALTIME_API=beta` is the fallback) — the owner's first browser test is step 6 with the first live call.

## Minutes and billing (step 5)

JobFlex pays Twilio and OpenAI; the company pays through its plan and minute packs.

- **Included minutes** — a plan limit, **AI desk minutes**, edited per plan in `/admin/plans` like any other (`planLimits LIMIT_DEFS deskMinutes`, monthly; the engine counts the desk's own calendar-month row, `limitsEngine countUsage`). A plan row that sets nothing gets the tier's default (`lib/aiDesk/shared DESK_MINUTES_DEFAULT`: Starter 0, Professional 150, Advanced 500; a build-your-plan company with the Phone page 60) — `lib/aiDesk/minutes includedDeskMinutes`. The owner sets the real numbers in the plan editor.
- **Packs** — 300 minutes for $29 (`DESK_PACK`), a balance that does not expire: `SyncState deskpacks:<orgId>` `{ seconds, autoTopUp, autoFailedAt, history[] }`. Included minutes go first; past them a call's seconds come off the packs when it ends (`settleDeskUse`, idempotent: the month's row remembers what it already took, `DeskUse.packSeconds`). `deskBalance` is the one figure everyone shows: included left + packs → *427 min left · 12 min left — running low · Out of minutes*.
- **Buying one** — `lib/aiDesk/packs`, the Lead Center's flow applied to one product with an inline price (no Stripe product to create): *Add 300 minutes — $29* → the card the subscription bills, "Charge $29 to Visa •4242?" → an off-session PaymentIntent (idempotent per company, pack count and minute); no card, a decline or 3-D Secure → Stripe Checkout (`?pane=desk&pack_paid=1&session_id=…` back, or the webhook `checkout.session.completed` with `jf_purpose: desk-pack` — whichever first; `addPackSeconds` lands a payment once by its id). Each pack: the balance, a `JobExpense` ("AI desk minutes", purpose `DESK`, $29, vendor JobFlex — the books call it that), a trail line.
- **Auto top-up** — a switch on the card (needs a card on file): when the balance is at or under 5 minutes after a call or test, `settleDeskUse` charges the card and emails the owner ("300 minutes added"); a refusal is kept (`autoFailedAt`), emailed, and not retried for a day.
- **Out of minutes** — the voice webhook (`lib/aiDesk/calls`): calls ring the company phone, or take a message — never the live desk — until minutes are added; `callBrief` refuses too (and caps every call at what is left, 60 s at least); "Test it here" is refused with the reason. The owner gets one email a month (`DeskUse.outNoticeAt`). The Phone page's line says it and offers *Add minutes*; the settings card shows the banner.
- Also fixed here: in *Ring my phone first*, a call nobody picked up now goes to the **live** desk (it used to get the recording); only a transfer the desk itself made never loops back (the call's `callout` row says so).
- The Stripe stand-in (`lib/sdk/stripeMock`) grew a card on the customer, off-session PaymentIntents and Checkout sessions, so the stand walks the whole purchase.
- Checks: `scripts/qa/ai-desk-step5.check.ts` (pure, 18: the rows, the balance math — overage never counted twice — the words), `scripts/qa/ai-desk-minutes.check.ts` (DB copy, 19: out → phone/message and the brief refused; a pack → the live desk, the cap; the settle once; a payment landing once; the out notice once and auto top-up failing honestly without a card; FORWARD's missed call → live desk, never after the desk's own transfer), the stand walk `walk-desk5.js` (26: the card, a charge to the card on file, auto top-up on, out of minutes on the settings/Phone page/webhook, auto top-up buying a pack when a settle runs, no card → Checkout → back paid, and cancelled).
- **Owner's side:** nothing to create on Stripe; set each plan's *AI desk minutes* in `/admin/plans` (else the defaults above apply).

## Going live (step 6)

What can be proven without the owner's accounts was; what needs them is now a checklist that reads the real state.

- **The rehearsal** — `cd voice && OPENAI_API_KEY=… node test/live.mjs` (`voice/README.md`): the real voice server against the real realtime API, a scripted caller spoken by the speech model and fed as Twilio's μ-law frames (`voice/test/audio.mjs`: G.711 both ways, WAV, a resampler — tested), turn-taking by listening for the desk's audio to stop. Verdicts: the desk speaks first and says it is a recorded virtual assistant, its audio is speech (RMS), the caller's words reach the transcript, open times then a booking for the caller, the goodbye ends the call, no realtime errors; the desk's voice is saved as a WAV. It needs a key at run time, so it is the owner's command — this machine has none, by design.
- **Twilio's protocol, reviewed against its reference** (`websocket-messages`): `start.customParameters.token`, inbound μ-law/8000 media, our `media`/`clear` back, `stop`, closing the socket → the `<Redirect>` after `<Connect>`. One change from it: the desk now hangs up on Twilio's **mark** echo — the goodbye actually played — with a 3-second fallback (`voice/call.mjs`; the fake client echoes marks).
- **`/admin/health` → AI desk · go live** (`lib/aiDesk/readiness.ts`, the card in `admin-health/health-content.tsx`): the OpenAI key and `VOICE_MODEL`; the voice server's `/health` fetched through `VOICE_SERVER_URL` (model/API shown, a mismatch with the app's `VOICE_MODEL` flagged); Twilio; Stripe; every company number as Twilio has it — its voice webhook against ours, with **Point it here** (`actions/adminAiDesk pointVoiceWebhook`) when it points elsewhere — who answers, minutes left; the platform's own lines too (the account's numbers no company registered — the texting number, the approved toll-free in the messaging service; each with a picker that **assigns** it to a company (`assignPlatformLine` → the same `voicenum:` index a bought number uses), because unassigned it falls back to the oldest company — a test call would land on their Phone page); each plan's included minutes (set, or the tier's default marked *); and *Still to do*, written from what is actually missing, ending with the rehearsal and the first real call.
- Walk: `walk-desk6.js` (7: the card, the voice server answering, the lights, the number, the plans, the steps).
